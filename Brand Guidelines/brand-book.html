Introduction

Why this brand exists

Have I Been Pwned was built so that anyone — not just security professionals — could find out if their personal data has been exposed in a data breach, for free. The brand has to carry that same ethos: no fear-mongering, no paywalls dressed up as "free", no corporate gloss. Just facts, delivered plainly.

Every design decision in this book is measured from the live product and the existing Figma source file, not invented. Where something hasn't been formalised yet, it's called out explicitly rather than guessed.

Free, always

The core checking service carries no cost and no dark patterns.

Transparent

Sources, numbers and methodology are shown, not hidden.

Pragmatic

Matter-of-fact tone over alarmism, even when the news is bad.

Credible

Built on data and evidence, not marketing claims.

Logo System

The symbol

The mark is a stylised rendering of ;-- — the syntax you'd use to break out of a SQL query in an injection attack. Five angled bars at a forward lean give it motion and an unmistakable silhouette at any size, from a 16px favicon to a wall print.

  • Always set at the same forward-leaning angle — never upright
  • Works as a single-colour mark in white, black or Pwned Blue
  • Used alone as the favicon, social avatar and app icon

Clear space & minimum size

Keep clear space on every side equal to the width of the tallest bar in the symbol. Never crowd the mark with text, UI chrome or imagery inside this zone.

Minimum size: 24px for the symbol alone on screen, 120px wide for the full wordmark lockup, 15mm wide in print.

Don't

Skew or stretch
Off-palette colour

Voice & Tone

How HIBP sounds

We are

  • Direct and matter-of-fact
  • Pragmatic, evidence-based and credible
  • Quietly witty, never forced
  • Transparent about what data is and isn't shown
  • User-first and free of sales pressure

We are not

  • Alarmist or fear-mongering
  • Corporate or jargon-heavy
  • Preachy about security hygiene
  • Salesy or hype-driven
  • Vague about sources or methodology
Use

pwned · breach · exposed · compromised · check · free · data

Avoid

hacked (sparingly) · victim (prefer affected/impacted) · revolutionary · best-in-class · seamless
